
Enterprise Process Lab Case Study
Problem

Enterprise implementations fail when procurement, finance, change-management, and approval systems do not integrate cleanly.

Design

Enterprise Process Lab models SAP-style procurement, Oracle Finance integration, ServiceNow change gating, Workday approval, controls compliance, and executive readiness.

Validation

Validated 4 integrations, 5+ controls, 24 tests, and release-blocking governance decisions.

Tradeoffs

Simulated enterprise workflow, but maps cleanly to analyze, design, build, test, implement, and support phases.
